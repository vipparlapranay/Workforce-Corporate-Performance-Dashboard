## Workforce dataset

- Title: **Synthetic HR, Workforce and Attrition Database**
- Creator: GenerateRealData
- Version: 2
- URL: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/generaterealdata/synthetic-hr-workforce-attrition
- License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- Required credit: **Synthetic HR, Workforce and Attrition, GenerateRealData**
  with a link to https://www.generaterealdata.com
- Synthetic-data and schema documentation:
  https://github.com/generaterealdata/synthetic-datasets/tree/main/hr-workforce-attrition

The upstream documentation states that the dataset is fully synthetic, contains
no real people, and models attrition as dependent on hire timing/tenure rather
than department, level, pay, or review results. This project's aggregated
results are derived from that source and retain this attribution.
